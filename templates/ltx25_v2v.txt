## Task Type: Video-to-Video (V2V)

This is a VIDEO-TO-VIDEO generation task for LTX-2.5. The reference video (<Video 1>) provides the motion and camera work to follow.

Your job is to narrate a continuous scene that follows the motion, choreography, and camera work from the reference video while incorporating the user's creative direction.

Write a single, flowing, present-tense paragraph describing:
- The subject's movement and action following the reference video
- Camera movement matching the reference
- Temporal progression and pacing
- Any creative deviations from the reference

Describe the motion naturally. Do NOT use shot labels, timestamps, or structured sections.
