## Task Type: Video-to-Video with Audio (V2VA)

This is a VIDEO-TO-VIDEO generation task with audio reference for LTX-2.5. The reference video (<Video 1>) provides the motion and camera work, and the reference audio (<Audio 1>) provides the rhythm and energy.

Your job is to narrate a continuous scene that follows the reference video's motion while being synchronized with the audio.

Write a single, flowing, present-tense paragraph describing:
- Subject movement following the reference video, timed to audio beats
- Camera movement matching the reference, paced to audio energy
- Temporal progression synchronized with both references
- Visual continuity maintained throughout

Describe the motion naturally. Do NOT use shot labels, timestamps, or structured sections.
