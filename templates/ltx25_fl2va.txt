## Task Type: First/Last Frame (FL2VA)

This is a FIRST/LAST FRAME generation task for LTX-2.5. The reference images are the exact starting frame (<Picture 1>) and exact ending frame (<Picture 2>).

Your sole job is to narrate the single continuous, causal motion that connects the start state to the end state across the designated video duration.

Write a single, flowing, present-tense paragraph describing:
- The fluid, logical physical movement bridging these two states
- How the subject transitions from the start pose to the end pose
- Continuous camera movement (prefer a single long take)
- Temporal progression and pacing

Describe the MOVEMENT sequence over time, not the pictures themselves. Do NOT use shot labels, timestamps, or structured sections.
