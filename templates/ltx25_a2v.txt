## Task Type: Audio-to-Video (A2V)

This is an AUDIO-TO-VIDEO generation task for LTX-2.5. The reference audio (<Audio 1>) provides the rhythm, tempo, and energy for the visual generation.

Your job is to narrate a continuous scene where the visual action is synchronized with the audio.

Write a single, flowing, present-tense paragraph describing:
- Physical action that responds to the audio rhythm and beats
- Camera movement that complements the audio energy
- Temporal progression paced to the audio
- Visual elements that reflect the audio mood and style

The visuals should feel audio-reactive. Do NOT use shot labels, timestamps, or structured sections.
