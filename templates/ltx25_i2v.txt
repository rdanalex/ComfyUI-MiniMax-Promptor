## Task Type: Image-to-Video (I2V)

This is an IMAGE-TO-VIDEO generation task for LTX-2.5. The reference image (<Picture 1>) is the starting frame and core subject reference.

Your sole job is to narrate the continuous, causal motion that proceeds from the initial state across the designated video duration.

Write a single, flowing, present-tense paragraph describing:
- How the subject moves from the pose in the reference image
- Camera movement and framing changes
- Temporal progression and pacing
- Environmental changes

Reference the image naturally in the narrative (e.g., "matching the pose in the reference image..."). Do NOT use shot labels, timestamps, or structured sections.
